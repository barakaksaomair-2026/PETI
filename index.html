Our Story

Engineering precision since 2013

Baraka Universal is a Saudi-based multi-discipline contracting firm delivering mission-critical projects for oil & gas, industrial and infrastructure clients across the Kingdom.

From electrical and instrumentation to civil, mechanical and IT — one trusted partner managing every discipline across the full project lifecycle, with a single line of accountability from mobilisation to final handover.

  • Single-contractor accountability across nine disciplines
  • ISO 9001, ISO 45001 and ISO 14001 aligned systems
  • Zero-harm HSE culture on every site
